# Acme Shop — Payments and Fulfilment Policy

Version 3, approved by the Operations Committee.

## Purpose

This policy protects our customers and keeps our books correct. Every team member is
expected to read it once a year and to treat customers politely.

## Definitions

An "order" is one purchase identified by its order number. A "refund request" is a
customer's written request for their money back.

## Payments

A customer must not be charged twice for the same order, unless the first charge was refunded.
Orders above 1000 USD need a manager approval before they are charged.

## Fulfilment

A cancelled order must never be shipped.
Every order must be charged before it is shipped.

## Refunds

A refund must be completed within 24 hours of the request.
The total refunded amount for an order must never exceed the total amount charged for it.

## Contact

Questions about this policy go to payments-policy@acme.example.
